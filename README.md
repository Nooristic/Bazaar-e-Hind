## Bazaar-e-Hind 🧵

A Digital B2B Marketplace for Indian Textiles

Bazaar-e-Hind is a secure, web-based B2B marketplace designed to digitalise the Indian textile industry. It eliminates traditional trade friction points—such as lack of transparency, geographical limitations, high intermediary dependence, heavy manual workflows, and fragmented communication—by providing a direct, centralized bridge between textile manufacturers and wholesalers across India.
------------------------------
## 🎯 Key Objectives

* Centralised Networking: Connects textile wholesalers and manufacturers across India on a single platform.
* Direct Fabric Sourcing: Enables wholesalers to source materials directly from producers at the click of a button.
* Real-Time Communication: Facilitates swift, direct interaction between stakeholders via dedicated channels.
* Exclusivity Management: Supports the creation, digital signing, and management of territory-specific business exclusivity agreements.
* Secure Credit Ecosystem: Provides a safe credit payment architecture utilizing UPI, cards, and netbanking.
* Disintermediation: Minimises reliance on manual tasks and traditional middlemen to boost profit margins.

------------------------------
## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Infrastructure

* Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+), Bootstrap
* Backend Environment: PHP / Apache (via XAMPP)

## 1. Admin Panel

* User Verification: Vets and verifies newly onboarded enterprise credentials based on roles and operational statuses.
* Content Moderation: Approves or rejects incoming manufacturer fabric listings before they go live to prevent unauthorized items.

## 2. Manufacturer Side

* Product Management: Lists public fabrics and handles restricted visibility for exclusive materials.
* Order Management: Reviews, confirms, or rejects bulk contract orders initiated by downstream wholesale buyers.
* Sample Management: Processes material sample requests and attaches courier tracking updates upon approval.
* Payment Ledger: Tracks ongoing payment transactions, credit histories, and outstanding invoices.
* Exclusivity Sign-Off: Reviews contract terms, grants regional exclusivity, and signs documents digitally.

## 3. Wholesaler Side

* Product Discovery: Filters and views validated, publicly available, non-exclusive textile supplies across India.
* Procurement Engine: Compiles material carts, processes sample demands, and dispatches bulk orders.
* Order Tracking: Monitors confirmed, pending, or historical transaction details.
* Credit Settlement: Settles invoices across delivery states (Confirmed, Dispatched, Delivered) based on specific credit terms.
* Exclusivity Drafting: Authors localized market agreements, appends digital signatures, and submits them for approval.
